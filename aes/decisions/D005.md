# D005 — the room and the contents now share a space, measured both ways

## Verdict: SUPORTADA

## The cause, and it was one line of placement

`RoomComponent.worldBounds` is the isometric projection — x from
`-(height-1) * tileWidth/2` to `(width-1) * tileWidth/2 + tileWidth` — which is the
space `gridToScreen` puts every entity in, and the space the camera frames.
`flame_tiled` puts a right-down isometric map at its own local origin, which is
the top corner of its bounding box. Same room, two systems, a room-width apart.

The fix places the tilemap at `worldBounds.topLeft`, read from the map rather than
written down as a constant.

## The falsifier, and its numbers

`room_placement_test.dart` removes every entity and both characters, renders
what is left, and calls that the room's box; then diffs the full frame against it
to get the contents' box, and requires the contents to be inside the room. The
diff is trustworthy because it restores what it removed and checks the frame came
back identical.

Before, with the map at its own local origin:

    room ink=232584 box=Rect.fromLTRB(571.0, 80.0, 1280.0, 720.0)
    contents ink=8656 box=Rect.fromLTRB(0.0, 120.0, 1160.0, 530.0)
      what is in the room is not inside the room, horizontally

After:

    room ink=410289 box=Rect.fromLTRB(0.0, 80.0, 1280.0, 720.0)
    contents ink=8656 box=Rect.fromLTRB(0.0, 120.0, 1160.0, 530.0)

The room's ink rose from 232,584 to 410,289 because it was half off the frame
before. The contents did not move — they were always in the right space; only
the map was in the wrong one.

Checked in a browser as well, because an off-screen test is what misled this
project twice today: the room is centred, fills the frame, and carries its wall
border and moss, where yesterday it ran off the right edge.

## What this verdict does not settle

Two things, both recorded as open rather than folded in.

The entities are drawn as short coloured bars, not as sprites. That is the second,
independent fault in D004 and this guard does not test size — it asks whether
they are in the room, not whether they look like anything. The off-screen harness
reports each entity painting 1,943 to 4,866 pixels, which is consistent with a bar.

And one observation I did not reproduce: after holding the virtual joystick for
two and a half seconds the room rendered empty — no entities, no party — while
the HUD still said `castle gatehouse`. It is in the board as an open question
because I saw it once and did not reproduce it, and one unreproduced observation
is not a finding.
