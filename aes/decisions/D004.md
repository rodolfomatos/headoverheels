# D004 — two coordinate systems, cause not yet discriminated

> **SUPERSEDED by `D005`.** This record said the cause could not be told from the
> symptom, and it could: two spaces were discriminated and the room was in the
> wrong one. It is left on file because the reasoning that stopped short is the
> useful part, but it is not a live answer. `scripts/aes_metrics.py conflict`
> fails while a superseded record stands unmarked.

## What is measured

In a 1000x720 window: the room occupies roughly x 470 to the right edge and runs
off it; sixteen entities appear as six-pixel squares between x 60 and x 660.

The page is cleared. `scripts/browser_canvas_geometry.js` against the HoH build:

    { "attrW": 1280, "attrH": 800, "cssW": 1280, "cssH": 800,
      "left": 0, "top": 0, "parent": "FLT-CANVAS-CONTAINER" }
    "dpr": 1

One canvas, 1:1, at the origin. The same measurement that cleared the Knight
Lore page in D001.

## Why this is NÃO-VERIFICÁVEL and not a guess

Two candidates, both consistent with what is on screen, and neither yet tested:

1. The camera is framed on `worldBounds`, and `worldBounds` is in
   `gridToScreen` space — x -512 to 512 for a sixteen-by-sixteen room — while
   the tilemap is placed in map-pixel space, x 0 to 1024. The room is then drawn
   a room-width to the right of everything else.
2. Both are in one space and the tilemap is placed correctly, and what is wrong
   is the entity sizing — they are six pixels where a tile is 64x32 — which
   would make the scatter look wrong without the room being misplaced at all.

Candidate 1 explains the room. Candidate 2 explains the squares. **They are
independent faults and both may be present**, so answering either one alone does
not close the ticket.

## What would settle it, and what it costs

Two reads and no build: `RoomDefinition.worldBounds`, to see which space it is
computed in, and the place the `TiledComponent` is added, to see whether a
position is set on it. Seconds.

Then a pixel measurement per object, which is the part no read settles: render
one room off screen with the camera composed, the way the Knight Lore guard now
does after D003, and assert that each entity's ink lands inside the room's ink
box. That guard is the thing this project did not have, and its absence is why
T078's "3,635 pixels painted" could be reported for a character that is nowhere
near the floor.

## Cost of being wrong here

Medium, and asymmetric. Guessing wrong costs another session of measuring; not
fixing it costs a game nobody can play, which is the state it is in now.
