# D003 — the guard now composes the camera, and fails when it is unpinned

## Verdict: SUPORTADA

`runtime_render_test.dart` rendered through `RoomView.render` and therefore
skipped the one transform the player never skips. It now applies the camera's
own transform before the view's, read from the camera rather than hard-coded, so
the two compose exactly as they compose in the game.

## The falsifier, and its output

The guard was run with the camera's viewfinder anchor put back to `center` —
the fault, deliberately:

    room: ink=205839 colours=111 box=[660, 468, 1280, 800]
          scale=2.400 offset=Offset(25.6, 73.6)
    margins: left=660.0 right=0.0 top=468.0 bottom=0.0
      the room is not centred horizontally: 660.0 and 0.0 to the edges of a
      1280x800 frame

Two things in that output are the finding, not the failure:

- `box=[660, 468, …]` is the same corner the ruler measured in a browser today,
  (660, 470), and the same corner the arithmetic predicted, (2560-1240)/2 = 660
  and (1600-664)/2 = 468. Three independent routes to one number.
- `offset=Offset(25.6, 73.6)` in the same line is the **view's** idea of where it
  drew the room. So one run reports two different answers to "where is the room",
  and only one of them is the player's. That is the blind spot, printed.

With the pin restored the same guard reports `box=[20, 68, 1260, 732]`, margins
20/20 and 68/68.

## Why `game.render` was not used instead

It is more faithful and it is not reachable here: `CameraComponent` renders the
world only when the world is mounted, a `GameWidget` is what mounts it, and the
widget's own load never returns in a widget test — measured, not assumed. Calling
`game.render(canvas)` from an unmounted game produced a blank frame, ink 0. So
the guard composes the two transforms by hand. It is still one layer short of the
full loop, and that limit is recorded here rather than glossed: a change to the
camera's *viewport*, as opposed to its viewfinder, would still pass this.

## What this verdict does not settle

The viewport half. `CameraComponent.renderTree` also translates by
`viewport.position - viewport.anchor * viewport.size` before the viewfinder's
transform, and the guard does not apply that. Today the viewport anchors at
top-left and sits at the origin, so it is a zero translate — but that is a
property of the current configuration, not something the guard enforces.
