# D007 — z is the height, and the tile plane is flat

## What was measured

A party nobody was touching slid south. Holding the right arrow for sixty frames
and releasing:

```
released at 2.87,28.77
after 60 more frames at 2.87,38.24
```

x frozen, y climbing at a rate that did not decay across a full second. Not
coasting.

## What was ruled out first

The conveyor. With `conveyor_1` removed from the room the party still walked, at
the same rate. The hypothesis T090 opened with was wrong, and saying so is part
of the record.

## What was actually happening

`CharacterStateNotifier._fixedUpdate` added `9.8 * dt * 60` to `velocity.y`, and
`y` is a tile row. `isGrounded` tested `z`, which nothing in that method wrote, so
the party was never grounded and `stop()` -- which clears the velocity -- was
overwritten on the next tick. `jump()` wrote its impulse into the same axis as a
negative `y`. The jump phases read `velocity.y > 0` for the apex, which on a tile
row means "moving south": the party peaked while falling and began falling while
rising.

Gravity and jump acted on the tile plane while `isGrounded`, `jumpPhase`,
`jumpHeight` and `position.z` all described a height axis that nothing integrated.
**The model did not say which axis was up.**

## The decision

`z` is the height, and the tile plane (x, y) is flat.

Not chosen by preference. The evidence pointed one way: `position` was already a
`Vector3`, `isGrounded` already tested `z`, `gridPosition` and the renderer
already treated x and y as tile coordinates, and every jump field already
described a height. `z` was the axis the model meant and the only one nothing
integrated.

Consequences, all of them obligations rather than conveniences:

- `verticalVelocity` is a new field on `CharacterState`, separate from `velocity`.
  A walk velocity and a vertical velocity are different quantities; putting them in
  one `Vector2` is what let gravity land in the tile plane.
- Nothing in this game moves the party along a row because it fell. If a future
  mechanic needs a slope, that is a new decision and it does not get to reuse this
  one's reasoning.
- Landing clamps `z` to zero and clears the vertical only. An earlier version
  cleared both axes and a party on the floor could not walk at all; the east test
  caught it at 0.03 tiles instead of 1.87.

## What this rests on

`keyboard_input_test.dart`: a held key moves the party the way the key points, a
released key stops it, and y holds while walking east. 117 tests in the package.
